What MediaGrab hosts

MediaGrab does not host, index or publish content. At a user's request it creates a temporary copy of publicly available content, which is deleted automatically within {{ settings.file_ttl_minutes }} minutes. There is no searchable library and no public file links.

  1. Your name and contact details
  2. A description of the copyrighted work
  3. The link(s) to the content on the original platform

  5. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner
