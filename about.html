MediaGrab is a free web app for saving public videos, photos and audio from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok — no account and no app install required.

What we stand for

Honest quality

We only list resolutions the source really offers. Nothing is upscaled into "fake 4K", and every finished file is verified with FFmpeg before it's handed to you.

Privacy first

No accounts, no tracking. Files are deleted from the server automatically after a short time, and the image converter runs entirely on your device.

Playing by the rules

We never bypass private accounts, DRM or paywalls. MediaGrab is for public content you own or have permission to use.

How it works

When you paste a link, the server first detects what it contains — a video, audio, photo, carousel, album, playlist or a mixed post — and shows only the options that genuinely exist. Downloads run in the background (pause, resume and retry are supported) and every file is verified at the end, so an incomplete download is never presented as a success.

MediaGrab is built on proven open-source tools: yt-dlp for extraction, FFmpeg for merging and conversion, gallery-dl for logged-in Facebook photos, and FastAPI for the server.

Who it's for

Creators backing up their own uploads, students and teachers keeping learning material for offline use, and anyone on a slow or expensive connection who wants to download once and watch many times.
